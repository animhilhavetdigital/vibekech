3-Day Sahara Desert Private Circuit
Our #1 recommended authentic Morocco experience: From Marrakech to Merzouga Dunes with Luxury Bivouac Camp.
D1

Day 1: Marrakech → High Atlas (Tizi n'Tichka) → Kasbah Ait Benhaddou → Dades Valley

Depart from Marrakech in a private air-conditioned 4x4 or Minivan. Cross the spectacular High Atlas mountain pass (2,260m) with panoramic stops. Explore the UNESCO World Heritage site of Kasbah Ait Benhaddou (Game of Thrones & Gladiator filming location). Continue through the Valley of Roses to spend the night in a charming Dades Valley Riad.

Tizi n'Tichka 2,260m Ait Benhaddou UNESCO Ouarzazate Dinner & Night in Dades Riad
D2

Day 2: Dades Gorges → Todra Canyons → Merzouga Dunes (Camel Trek & Luxury Camp)

After breakfast, explore the towering 300m limestone cliffs of Todra Gorges. Head towards the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi, Merzouga. Mount your camel for a 1-hour sunset trek into the heart of the Sahara. Arrive at your private luxury desert bivouac, enjoy traditional Berber music by the campfire, and sleep in a heated tent with private bathroom.

Todra Gorges Sunset Camel Trek Luxury Camp with Shower Berber Music & Stargazing
D3

Day 3: Sahara Sunrise → Draa Valley Palms → Ouarzazate → Return to Marrakech (or Fez)

Wake up early for an unforgettable sunrise over the golden sand dunes. Enjoy a rich Moroccan breakfast, then journey back through the lush Draa Valley with thousands of date palm trees, returning safely to your Riad in Marrakech (or optional drop-off in Fez).

Dune Sunrise Draa Valley Palm Oasis Hotel/Airport Drop-off

Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about booking transfers, desert tours, and private excursions with GlobeTrek.
How does the airport transfer pickup work?
Our driver will wait for you directly at the Marrakech Menara Airport arrival terminal holding a sign with your name. We track your flight in real time, so even if your flight is delayed, your driver will be there at no extra charge. If your Riad is inside the car-free Medina, our driver will accompany you or coordinate with your Riad staff to assist with your luggage.
Are the desert tours and excursions 100% private?
Yes! All our tours and circuits are 100% private for you and your group. You will have your own dedicated modern vehicle (4x4 or Minivan) and professional driver, allowing you to stop whenever you want for photos, coffee, or sightseeing at your own pace.
What is your cancellation and modification policy?
We offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before the scheduled departure for most day trips and airport transfers. Date and itinerary changes are completely free of charge upon availability.
What should I pack for the Sahara Desert & Agafay?
We recommend bringing comfortable clothing, sunglasses, sunscreen, a hat/scarf for the sun, and a warm jacket for the evening, as desert temperatures drop after sunset. All bedding and towels are provided at our luxury camps.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept secure online credit card payments, PayPal, bank transfers, or cash on arrival (in Moroccan Dirhams, Euros, USD, or British Pounds).
Can we customize the departure time or add extra stops?
Absolutely! Because your trip is private, you can request custom pickup times, add visits to specific Berber villages, artisan cooperatives, or scenic viewpoints. Simply let your driver or our WhatsApp support know.
